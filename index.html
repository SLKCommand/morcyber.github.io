FAQ

Common questions

Is a risk analysis actually required?

Yes. The HIPAA Security Rule requires covered entities and business associates to conduct an accurate and thorough assessment of the risks to ePHI and to implement measures that reduce them. See HHS guidance.

How often should we do one?

HIPAA does not set a fixed interval; HHS describes risk analysis as an ongoing process. Many practices review annually and after major changes such as a new EHR, a new location, or a security incident.

Should we start with the Snapshot or the Full Assessment?

The Snapshot is a fast, low-cost look at your paperwork and staff practices. The Full Assessment is the thorough risk analysis the Security Rule calls for. We’ll recommend one on your free consultation.

Will you certify us as HIPAA compliant?

No. There is no official government HIPAA certification, and no consultant can guarantee compliance. We give you a documented assessment and a clear plan to reduce risk.

Do you need access to patient records?

We assess systems, policies, and safeguards, not patient charts. If an engagement may involve access to PHI, we’ll put a Business Associate Agreement in place first.
